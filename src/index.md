I'm a PhD candidate in the Department of Biomedical Engineering at Johns Hopkins University, advised by Joshua Vogelstein, Pratik Chaudhari, and Carey Priebe. My research focuses on learning from non-stationary data, culminating in a mathematical framework called *prospective learning*.

I received an M.S.E. in Applied Mathematics and Statistics from Johns Hopkins (2024) and a B.Sc. in Biomedical Engineering from the University of Moratuwa, Sri Lanka (2020).
